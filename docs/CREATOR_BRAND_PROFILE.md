# Creator Brand Profile — v0.1 (2026-11-24)
_Evolving document. Brand name and handles are TBD; nothing in templates may hard-code them._

## Identity
- **Personality:** Warm, playful Gujju girl with effortless style (provisional, review after first drafts)
- **Anchor reference:** Rajvee Gandhi (@rxjvee): fashion/lifestyle/festive styling, playful Hinglish tone. Study her techniques; never copy her content.
- **Content categories:** dance, daily-life vlogs, Garba/Dandiya/Navratri, concerts and events, travel and outings, fashion and lifestyle, friends and celebrations, trending audio, Gujarati personality storytelling
- **Accounts:** not created yet. Content-first backlog strategy.

## Language
- Gujarati + Hinglish mix
- Spoken Gujarati: transcribed faithfully, never reworded or invented. Ask when unsure.
- Captions: English transliteration for body captions; Gujarati script for short hero text
- Caption tone: warm, playful, witty Hinglish with Gujarati flavour words

## Visual identity
| Content type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Daily vlogs | Relatable, soft-cinematic, natural tones, gentle motion |
| Dance / Garba / Concerts | Energetic, cinematic, beat-synced cuts, richer festive colour |
| Fashion / Occasions | Elegant, glamorous, clean type, slower polished motion |
- Constant across all: font family, palette, caption style (to be designed in the Style Bible)
- Typography (proposed): Gujarati-capable OFL fonts (Hind Vadodara / Mukta Vaani / Noto Gujarati) + a premium Latin display font. Render test required.
- Brand colours: TBD (to come from the Phase 4 research and the first drafts)

## Voiceover
- Her own voice only. Each edit ships with a **VO script**: timed lines, Hinglish/Gujarati, delivery notes (energy, pauses), target duration.
- No synthetic or cloned voice and no likeness alteration.

## Music
- Licensed music only in exported files. Trending platform audio is added in-app at posting time (noted in the publish package).

## Do-not-use
- No user-imposed creative restrictions.
- Baseline guardrails: natural appearance, faithful Gujarati, licensed music, culturally respectful.
