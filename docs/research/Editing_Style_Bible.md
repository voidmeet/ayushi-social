# Editing Style Bible — v0.1 (2026-11-24)
_Evolves with each research batch and with performance data. Grounded in 89 verified references; see `Top_50_Editing_Techniques.md`._
Brand personality: **Warm, playful Gujju girl with effortless style.** Original identity, inspired by but never copying Rajvee Gandhi and peers.

## 1. Non-negotiables (every edit)
- **Frame 0 is the thumbnail**: subject visible, mid-action or mid-expression; never black, never an empty set.
- **Hook within 1 s**: text hook and/or visual hook (tease, reaction, reveal setup).
- **9:16, 1080×1920**; no letterboxing; all text inside Reels/Shorts safe zones (HyperFrames check).
- **Natural appearance**: no face reshaping or heavy smoothing; grade colour, not faces.
- **Faithful Gujarati**: her speech never reworded; captions reviewed before burn-in.
- **True-peak limiter at −1 dBTP**, loudness ≈ −14 LUFS.

## 2. Typography system (draft)
| Role | Style | Font |
|---|---|---|
| Hero line (Gujarati) | Short, 2–5 words, large, warm accent colour | Hind Vadodara Bold |
| Hook / caption pill | Small white rounded pill, top-centre, sentence case + 1 emoji | Latin sans (TBD) |
| Editorial accent | Serif italic word or line (fashion/occasion edits) | Playfair Display Italic |
| Title signature | Handwritten/script title + small cut-out sticker of her (travel/outfit series) | Script font (TBD, OFL) |
| Split labels | White labels above halves (e.g. "Basic / Better") | Latin sans |
| Body subtitles | Transliterated Hinglish/Gujarati, word-timed, lower third avoiding face | Latin sans |

## 3. Colour
- **Festive (garba/Navratri/weddings):** warm rich reds, golds, magentas; lifted saturation in costumes, protected skin.
- **Daily vlog:** natural, soft-cinematic, gentle warmth; no heavy LUTs.
- **Fashion/occasion:** clean, slightly warm; optional cream editorial palette for premium posts.
- **Night/concert:** keep stage colour, prevent skin crush; mild contrast.

## 4. Per-content-type recipes
| Type | Length | Signature moves |
|---|---|---|
| Solo garba / dance | 7–15 s | Tease caption → beat drop; cut on spin peaks; black-drape or night-ground setting; 1 slow-mo highlight max |
| Navratri series | 15–30 s | "Routine for these 9 days" listicle; day→night transitions; Gujarati hero line |
| Outfit / look | 10–20 s | Hold-up → wearing reveal; bare face → full look match-cut; locked-tripod multi-look; Basic vs Better |
| Concert / event | 15–30 s | "What I wore to…" intercut; silhouette crowd shots; "two types of people" caption |
| Daily vlog | 30–60 s | Talk-to-camera + quick B-roll; golden-hour closer; her own VO from script |
| Personality | 15–30 s | Gujarati family characters, sibling/friend banter; punch-in on punchline |

## 5. Transitions (use with intent, max 2 styles per edit)
Match-cut (same framing) · hold-up reveal · hand-cover swipe · twirl/spin cut · day→night · zoom-blur in. Avoid stacking glitch/flash transitions.

## 6. Open questions for later batches
Audio (music styles, SFX density, beat-sync rate), exact cut frequency and caption animation can't be observed from frames. They need audio-level research or her own analytics after launch.
