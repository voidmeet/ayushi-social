# Gujarati Creator Trend Report — v0.1 (2026-11-24, batch 1)
Based on 89 verified YouTube Shorts references (view counts captured 2026-11-24). Correlation, not causation; trends are *observed patterns*, not guarantees.

## Observed patterns
1. **Transformation content dominates fashion.** The highest-fit references (Rajvee, Komal Pandey, Mrunal Panchal, Grincy Gandhi, StyleYourAura) rely on a look *change*: hold-up → wear, bare → glam, casual → lehenga, basic → better.
2. **Navratri content is listicle- and mood-driven.** "Routine for these 9 days", rain-garba mood pieces and "wait for the pickup" beat drops beat plain dance captures.
3. **Gujarati script is used as a hook, not as subtitles.** Native one-liners (રમતા કાના ને રાધા…, સાસુ-વહુ-દીકરી) sit at the top as the premise; body text stays Hinglish/English. This matches our caption decision.
4. **Wedding/sangeet dance is the biggest reach category** (multi-million views), driven by emotional reactions and surprise reveals more than choreography quality.
5. **Concert Shorts that travel are framed**: comparison captions, outfit intercuts, or a single spectacle moment, not raw fan-cam.
6. **Clean minimal captions beat heavy effects.** Top creators use one small caption pill plus strong visuals; face filters and comedy effects appear in low-fit, off-brand content.
7. **Sets are simple but intentional**: black drapes, marigold curtains, fairy lights, clean walls, golden hour.

## What this means for our creator
- Her **Navratri 2026 footage** suits: a 9-nights listicle, a rain/mood piece (if footage allows), solo garba with tease → drop, and Navratri look transitions. Hold as throwback/next season.
- Her **daily vlog footage** suits: talk-to-camera + B-roll with her own VO, "outfits I wore in…", golden-hour closers.
- **Avoid** face-effect comedy, pranks and generic stage captures.

## Data gaps
Garba (9/40) and concerts (5/40) are under-sampled because Gujarati creators publish mainly on Instagram. Next batch: YouTube Data API key + targeted channels + user-shared Instagram links.
